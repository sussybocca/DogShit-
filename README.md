# DogShit Genesis — The Ultimate Cartoon Chaos Sandbox 💥

**Welcome to DogShit Genesis, a wildly unpredictable 2D physics sandbox where chaos, creativity, and ridiculous cartoon physics collide!**

Explore a living city populated by autonomous NPCs with unique personalities, behaviors, and pre-recorded AI-generated voices. Experiment with exaggerated ragdoll physics, destroy buildings, launch characters across the map, manipulate gravity, and turn an ordinary city into a hilarious playground of possibilities.

Featuring an expanding library of nearly **500 original AI-generated sound effects**, thousands of NPC dialogue recordings, destructible environments, customizable physics, and an integrated Mod Studio, DogShit Genesis gives players the freedom to create their own ridiculous scenarios.

**Key Features**
- 🌆 **Living 2D City** — Explore interactive districts filled with autonomous NPCs and dynamic environments.
- 💥 **Destruction & Ragdoll Physics** — Break buildings, launch objects, manipulate gravity, and create spectacular cartoon chaos.
- 🗣️ **Voiced NPCs** — Characters feature distinct personalities, behaviors, and locally generated voice recordings, with configurable simultaneous dialogue limits.
- 🔊 **Original Sound Library** — Hundreds of unique cartoon impacts, musical footsteps, environmental sounds, and exaggerated physics effects.
- 🔫 **Delete Gun & Population Control** — Remove, restore, and manage individual NPCs or entire districts.
- 🛠️ **Mod Studio** — Customize physics, NPC behavior, and gameplay systems while preserving the original character assets.
- 💾 **World Saving** — Save your sandbox experiments, settings, and population changes.
- 🌐 **Fully Offline Standalone Edition** — Package the entire game, including its sprites, code, dialogue, and sound effects, into a single HTML file with no server or internet connection required.

**No mandatory cloud AI. No required online servers. Just pure, ridiculous sandbox freedom.**

*DogShit Genesis — Break the physics. Not the fun.*
